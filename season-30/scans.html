what are SCANS (to me)?

SCANS will be the "daily sports segment" of the "idea"/show (SEASON 30)

Not necessarily formal, but things I feel could potentially pass on actual local news shows in terms of subject material

Mostly videos / material that might be helpful to a "beginner" or someone watching a WNBA (or basketball game) for the first time

Using scans to organize it in my mind as videos based on explaining things relating to "common assumptions" (or definitive facts) of basketball/the WNBA

Material is specific to one specific subject within the same time period (relatively)... Like recapping games over a week, a special event, giving facts about one specific player or team... Recent developments... Updates....

Slightly "deeper" than one item within a reference set (like a definition of "layup" or "rebound") but is surface level assessments/looks at things...(not opinionated // complex // etc......)

the starting point for this project... easy and quick things I know I can put together and feel most confident in !!
