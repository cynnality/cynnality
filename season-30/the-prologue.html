WNBA Connections

D.T. and the Mercury

Taurasi was the senior-most Olympic and WNBA player on the 2024 roster and with the win in Paris, she became the only player in Olympic basketball history to have 6 gold medals. She was drafted into the WNBA in 2004 by the Phoenix Mercury and stayed with them her entire WNBA career up until her retirement after the 2024 season. Not only was she prominent globally at the Olympics and nationally within the U.S. - she was also a huge player in Russia. She played for a professional Russian team during her off-seasons and even sat out of the 2015 WNBA season to play for the Russian team because they paid her significantly more than the WNBA. She won 3 WNBA championships with the Mercury. The first in 2007 and the second in 2009. For her third championship, she'd have the help of future Olympic teammate - Brittney Griner.

Diana Taurasi

WNBA Connections

Powerhouse Duo, D.T. and B.G.

Brittney Griner was drafted into the WNBA in 2013 and she'd end up with Taurasi in Phoenix. The Mercury, with Taurasi and Griner on the team, went on to win the WNBA championship the next year in 2014. Brittney Griner is THE dunker of the WNBA. She wasn't the first (or second) to dunk in the W but she BY FAR has done it the most.

She's probably the first WNBA player I learned about. way before I started watching basketball, in 2022 when I heard news of her arrest and imprisonment in Russia. in 2015, Griner started playing for the same Russian team Taurasi had been playing for, UMMC Ekaterinberg. In February of 2022, Griner was arrested in Russia at an airport for having a weed pen. She had to face trial and was sentenced to nine years, despite the typical Russian punishment for the crime being under a year. The U.S. government stepped in and negotiated with the Russian's to get Griner back. The U.S. and Russian governments agreed to a prisoner swap - the U.S. released a Russian arms dealer and Russia released Brittney Griner in December of 2022. She missed the entire 2022 season due to this imprisonment but returned in 2023. She played her 11th season with the Mercury in 2024 and made her first team-move for the 2025 season when she went to the Atlanta Dream.

The 2025 season would be the first WNBA season in over 20 years without the massively influential and all time great, Diana Taurasi. The subsequent exit of Brittney Griner, meant the Mercury would have to play without the powerhouse duo of D.T. and B.G. and the season ahead was looking scary for Phoenix. Luckily for them, a powerhouse player, nicknamed The Engine_ was looking for a new team...

Brittney Griner

WNBA Connections

The Engine

Alyssa Thomas, or _The Engine_, joined the Phoenix Mercury for the 2025 season after playing for the Connecticut Sun for 10 years. She was drafted in 2014 by the Sun and didn't make the playoffs until 2017 when she made her first playoff appearance, something the Sun hadn't done since 2012. She actually played her first post-season game against the Phoenix Mercury and lost.

In my eyes, A.T. is the all around best player without a championship ring yet. She got super close to getting her first champ title in 2025 - making it to the 2025 playoff finals, but the Aces ultimately ended up with the win.

Alyssa Thomas

WNBA Connections

Gray: Mastermind and Point Gawd

Chelsea Gray was on the Aces team that defeated Alyssa Thomas and the Mercury in the 2025 finals. Both Gray and Thomas entered the league in 2014 and they both went to the Connecticut Sun! The universe knew these 2 would be too powerful together so although they have almost identical introductions to the WNBA, the paths of their careers within the league have been very different.

Chelsea Gray came into her pro career with an injury so she sat out for her rookie season but she saw some game time for the Sun in 2015. In 2016, Gray left the Sun for the Sparks in Los Angeles. She'd win the championship title her first year with the Sparks and played her last season in L.A. in 2020. She went to the Las Vegas Aces in 2021, joining A'ja Wilson, Jackie Young, and Kelsey Plum. Not only would these 4 players win back-to-back WNBA championship titles (2022, 2023) - they'd also go on to form 1/3 of the 2024 Olympic team USA roster.

Chelsea Gray is nicknamed the Point Gawd so there's no doubt she's a bucket. But she's also just on a completely different level when it comes to her court-vision, passing abilities, and leadership in general. She suffered a season-ending injury in 2023 when the Aces made it to the playoffs, giving them the opportunity to defend their 2022 title. To offset her inability to participate on the court, she took on a leadership role, effectively becoming an assistant coach, and helped the Aces take the repeat title in 2023.

Chelsea Gray, Kelsey Plum, A'ja Wilson, and Jackie Young (all on team USA 2024 roster) form what I like to call the "Core Four". This group lasted from 2021-2024 because in 2025 Kelsey Plum went to the Sparks and the Aces picked up 2 new players...

Chelsea Gray

Luck the Jewell

Loyd was drafted in 2015 by the Seattle Storm. She won the 2018 and 2020 WNBA championships with the Storm and stayed with them until 2024. She started playing for the Aces along with Dana Evans in 2025. Together, they joined the remaining members from the broken-up "Core Four" - Chelsea Gray, A'ja Wilson, and Jackie Young - and the group was reinvented into the "Lucky Five". They are the ones, along with the rest of the Aces team, that won the 2025 Championship title. (so much more to say.... rough drafting for now so will add later)

WNBA Connections

Stewie Arrives

Breanna Stewart was drafted a year after Loyd, in 2016. She also went to the Seattle Storm. Stewie and Loyd got their first and second championship titles together as teammates and they'd both earn their third separately with different teams. We already know Jewell won her third with the Aces. Stewie won hers with the New York Liberty in 2024. Although Stewie had already had 2 rings, the New York Liberty had never been the WNBA championship team. In the 2024 Finals, the Liberty were up against the Minnesota Lynx - a team that had won the WNBA championship title 4 times already.



Sabrina Ionescu was drafted in 2019 by the Liberty. She won the 2024 Championship title with Breanna Stewart!

WNBA Connections

Kah's in the Sky

Drafted in 2016, same year as Breanna Stewart! Kah went to the Washington Mystics for her rookie WNBA year but she wouldn't be there long. In 2017 she joined the Sky in Chicago. 4 years later in 2021 - the Sky won the WNA Championships and Kah won finals MVP. She left Chicago after the 2022 season and in 2023 started playing for the Phoenix Mercury - joining B.G. and D.T.. She stayed after Griner and Taurasi left and was joined by A.T. in 2025, making it all the way the 2025 WNBA finals. This 2025 finals matchup had 5 2024 team USA roster players on the court - 3 for the Aces and 2 for the Mercury.

Kahleah Copper
