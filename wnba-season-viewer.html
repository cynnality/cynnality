Before you explore

This page is a major work in progress.

Working on this page requires the navigation of so many different challenges. Not only am I trying to visually display a BUNCH of data in a way that's easy to understand but I'm also running into the unexpected issue of discerning what should and should not be displayed and how to visually represent and organize it.

What should go here and what should go on a dedicated page?
What elements/details are useful to me but not necessarily contributing to the overall goal of this page?

These are the types of challenges I LOVE working on. At the same time, this page can periodically become a bit daunting. I'm taking a more delicate approach to this page since it carries a bit more significance to me than the average page.

For now - the dedicated viewer pages are probably a better place to actually read and take in the information on this page. But please don't let that detour you from looking around anyways!
