# What is Sixth Player of the Year? 

There's always 10 players on a basketball court at any given point of a game. 5 players for team A and 5 players for team B.

When the game starts, the first 5 players on the court for their team are known as the _starters_ 

Most of the time, the _starters_ need a break at point in the game. The coaches decide what players _come off the bench_ and _sub in_ for players currently on the court. **Sixth Player** refers to the player that the coach almost always picks first to _sub in_ for a starter. The term "back up" doesn't really do it justice because it's pretty much unheard of for all 5 starting players to stay on the court for every minute in a game... and "back up" makes it seem like a one-off, uncommon occurrence... Coaches **know** that certain players perform better as starters and others perform better as players that come off the bench... So in my mind "clutch player" is more fitting than "back up"... it's the player(s) that the coach keeps in their metaphorical back pocket as a "special weapon" of sorts... so Sixth Player of the year is a player that performed the best as their teams "special weapon" (in my opinion)
