Angel Reese's Crown and the team in CHI-TOWN

June 10, 2025

The Chicago Sky faced a tough loss against the New York Liberty - 66 to 85 - on Tuesday (6/10/25). At Chicago's post-game press conference, veteran player Ariel Atkins spoke up for Angel Reese after more than a few irelevant questions were thrown Reese's way.

At the post-game press conference, Reese was asked a question that referenced the scrutiny she receives online and she replied, saying it's not something she pays attention to. She said she's never once had a negavtive interaction with a fan in person and the people behind keyboards are there for a reason. Reese ended by saying she knows her teammates and coaches trust her and have her back. And to her, that's all that matters.

Before moving on to the next question Ariel Atkins asked to speak. Here's what she had to say:

“Angel Reese is a 23-year-old kid. And with the amount of crap she gets on a day-to-day basis, she still shows up. So if you have questions about our team or basketball, we appreciate it. But all the other nonsense is irrelevant. We’re here to play basketball. Yeah, we’re having a hard time right now at the end of the day. But

Angel Reese is a 23-year-old kid who handles herself with grace. Her crown is heavy.

People come at her for the way she acts or the way she builds up a wall. She has to build the wall. Because if she doesn't, people will break her down — not only for the way she looks, but the way she carries herself. She doesn't just walk around and act like "Oh I'm this". No. She knows who she is. And we are not going to break her down like that.

So whatever other questions y’all have, I'm hoping it's just about basketball. Maybe a box-score question. But the kid’s crown is heavy. Like... respect that.”

— Ariel Atkins, Chicago Sky

This 2025 season is Ariel's 8th WNBA season and her first season playing with the Chicago Sky after spending her first seven seasons with the Washington Mystics. She is an olympic gold medalist, a FIBA world cup champion and won a WNBA championship in 2019. She is the first and only player in the WNBA to make an All-Defensive team in each of her first five seasons.

During her 2023-2024 off-season, she signed on as an assistant player development coach for the Michigan Wolverines basketball team and helped them make it to the 2024 NCAA tournament. In an article about her experience coaching, Atkins said it helped her learn to talk in uncomfortable situations, how to connect with people, and build relationships. She cared about how the players felt and would ask them questions about their overall experience outside of basketball because she understands how certain things unrelated to basketball can translate to the court.

Ariel's approach to basketball in general showed up so clearly when she spoke up for Angel during the presser. She didn't defend stats or debate character - she stated a boundary, spoke up for Angel's humanity, and immediately shifted the tone of the room.

On Monday June 9 2025, the night before this game, Angel Reese was named BET's sportswoman of the year for the third consecutive year in a row. Angel won the award for the first time in 2023 as a college student after helping the LSU basketball team program win their first NCAA championship in a game that broke several NCAA Championship records. Angel was also named the Most Outstanding Player out of the final four teams at that 2023 championship tournament. She became Sportswoman of the Year for a second time in 2024 during her rookie WNBA season. Angel gets the title again for the third time this year after consistently being in the league's top 5 rebounders, despite the Chicago Sky's struggles so far this season. She is the only athlete besides Serena Williams to be named Sportswoman of the Year three times.

She is the most followed player in the WNBA with 5 million + followers on Instagram (more than the WNBA itself). She started her own foundation that helps empower young girls and women through sports, education, and financial literacy. She was given keys to the city of Baltimore from Mayor Brandon Scott in 2023. She is the first woman to have a Mcdonald's meal named after her. She partnered with cashapp to revitalize a community basketball court in Baton Rouge. She even has her own merch line and I want literally every item. All this and it's only her second year playing professionally.
