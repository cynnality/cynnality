<div class="history-sec">
		<h2>
			History and background of India and Pakistan conflicts
		</h2>
	<p><u>Jammu and Kashmir</u> is home to both <u>Hindus</u> and <u>Muslims</u> and has served as the battle ground for the animosities between the 2 religious groups. The trouble reportedly starts when Britain implemented a plan to split India up into 2 separate and independent nations. Under this plan - India would become India <em>and</em> Pakistan. Pakistan was meant to be the "Muslim Homeland". India was predominantly Hindu but was meant to be a "secular homeland". The region of Jammu and Kashmir was allowed to choose which nation to join - Pakistan or India. Instead, the people of this region held out for their own independence. </p>
</div>
	<div class="bullet-list">
	<div class="bullet">
		<h3>
			War breaks out
		</h3>
		<p>
Ultimately, Pakistan ended up backing <em>militants</em> from the Jammu and Kashmir region in their plans to revolt against the government. The Jammu and Kashmir government ended up asking India for help to stop the violence being caused by the Pakistani back militants in the region. The stage is now set as India and Pakistan help opposite sides of the "battle" - leading up to the first war between India and Pakistan in October of 1947.
		</p>
	</div>
	<div class="bullet">
		<h3>
			War breaks out again
		</h3>
		<p>
			In 1965, war between India and Pakistan breaks out again when <u><strong>armed Azad Kashmir troops</strong></u> from Pakistan invaded India. The Pakistani side of the border between Pakistan and India is referred to as the <mark>Azad Kashmir</mark>.
		</p>
	</div>
	<div class="bullet">

			1971 - Pakistan accuses India of aiding <u>East Pakistan</u> or <u>Bangladesh</u> in its quest for independence. Pakistan retaliated by invaded Jammu and Kashmir.
		</p>
