**_academic_ frames of reference**
- ==Classical theory== -> concepts are essentially definitions characterized by fixed rules determining to which entities the concept applies
- ==Prototype theory== -> argues that concept membership depends on similarity to a prototype - a cluster of features typically associated with the category
- ==Exemplar Theory== -> says similarity derives from individual memories of concrete instances rather than a single prototypical summary representation
- ==Theory theory== -> says concepts are embedded in domain-specific theories
- ==Conceptual Atomism== -> argues that lexical concepts are separate units without internal structure
- There are debates about whether concepts are mental representations or abstract objects that exist independently of individual minds.
