# The Agreement

> In order for one person to effectively communicate to another person, they must agree on the delivery system of their communication. I like "agreement" in this sense because if one person has power over another, the conditions required for an agreement to take place can't be met.  "Agreement" signals towards _compromise_. I interpret those 2 words to represent a setting where all parties make collective decisions on how they will communicate to ultimately understand each other better. 

Trying to understand what I feel separates "genuine" and "deceptive" communication
basically arguing for what is a truth and a lie... lol

---
- truth/natural/equal/collaborative 
- genuine attempts to convey/express oneself with words with intentions or hopes of being understood by another/others 
- **act of expressing being the motivating factor for the attempt**
- using meaning to collaboratively shape more and more meaning to form a common base
---
- deceptive/calculated/control 
- mimics attempts of genuine expressions with intentions of eliciting a desired response/outcome/result (taking advantage of the agreement ?)
- manipulating meaning as a way to mechanize the communication process 

- "Speaking from the heart" vs. "selling" to someone
