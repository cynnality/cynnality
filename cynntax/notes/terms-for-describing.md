**Entity**
A thing that *exists* in a more *concrete* sort of way but not necessarily in a physical/tangible sort of way

- is "tangible" or "identifiable"
- "*concrete*" and/or "*clearly defined*" opposed to subjective / abstract
examples: team, league, player, contract, salary cap

**Process**
A thing *over a period of time*

- Or observations on an *action*. Then observing the outcomes/consequences of that *action* (?)
- verbs / actions / dynamics
- it "*occurs*" and/or *"unfolds"*
examples: negotiation, growth, exploitation, expansion

**Mechanism**
How a *thing* works or how it *interacts* (?) 

- thinking "knee jerk" / "automatic" type of *sequences* that involve more than one singular *thing* 
- Similar to "process" in that it *might* involve time - but is primarily focused on the *interactions* of the *thing*
- *Causal structures* involving:
- more than 1 *thing*
- *hierarchical structure* (most commonly) 
- "plinko" - "tracking" the way the "chip" falls within the larger *mechanism* (showing or portraying the *levels* of the mechanism and how the *mechanism works*

**Strategy**
Intentional plan by *thing* (or *actor*)

- deliberate / *goal-oriented* behavior
- *planned pattern of action* related to *power* / *goals*

**Structure**
The *system* (or maybe *thing*?) itself

- looking at the *environment* / *setup* of a *thing*
- mechanism would be looking at how all the parts of a PC connect, interact, and work together and what the outcome is at different points
- *structure* would be looking at the general ways in which PC's work



##### *Mechanism* // *Strategy* // *Structure* (compare & contrast)

**Narrative / Frame**
How a *thing* is explained or justified

- the language used to explain a *thing* which then -> **shapes perception**

**Outcome**
The *results* of *thing*

- *Measurable* results (?)
