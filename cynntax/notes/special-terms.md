# _Where_ and _when_ do special terms belong?

I completely understand the value and function of specialized terminology. 
But I believe their function _only_ exists within "mastery" levels of a subject/field and have little to no value when used _outside_ those "mastery" levels.


- Example: The medical term for "heart attack" has very little significance to people who are not specialized in the field of medicine -> So when speaking to a person outside of the "core" of the medical field, using the term "myocardial infarction" would only obscure the overall "message" - **even if the use of the term _is_ factually correct** 
- People who are inside the "core" medical field (and regularly say/hear "myocardial infarction") might _overtime_ lose a sense of _how specialized_ the term really is due to their proximity to that specialization (i.e. they might be "surprised" by someone that _doesn't know_ what the term means) -> Thus they lose awareness for the totality of the knowledge base they rely on within their specialization (i.e. they **do not** have to explicitly _think_ about the fact that myocardial infarction = heart attack and vice versa). The unit of _specialized knowledge_ (myocardial infarction) turns into an _assumption_ which is relied upon in an automatic, seemingly instantaneous way
- This automatic reliance on specialized knowledge in this way is essential to lots of things in human life. People in the medical field _need_ to know specialized terms in order to be effective in their communication with their counterparts and carrying out tasks related to their role. 
- The issue is the misconception (?) that existence within the "core" of a specialized field adequately signals (positively) towards a person's ability to effectively _teach_ and/or _explain_ aspects of that specialized field (_especially to newcomers/beginners/students/etc.)

#### defining vs. explaining

- Definition provides information to the extent of a thing _in a vacuum (stripped of context and meaning) [a unit of information, suspended in space // no reference as to placement  / use in larger contexts]
- _Explaining_ provides the same info as a definition AND ADDS indicators and references to it's placement in relation to other things
