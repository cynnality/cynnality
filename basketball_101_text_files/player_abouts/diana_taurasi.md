## Diana Taurasi

<p class="olympic-detail>Taurasi was the senior-most Olympic and WNBA player on the 2024 roster and with the win in
Paris, she became the only player in Olympic basketball history to have 6 gold medals.</p>

<p> She was drafted into the WNBA in 2004 by the Phoenix Mercury 
and stayed with them her entire WNBA career up until her retirement after the 2024 season.
</p>
