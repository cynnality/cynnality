Final Injury Report for Lynx-Wings: Will Azzi Fudd, Napheesa Collier Play?

Nathaniel Holloway

The Dallas Wings are at home on Thursday night as they host the Minnesota Lynx in a Western Conference matchup.

Dallas enters Thursday’s game with a 1-1 record after losing at home to the Atlanta Dream on Tuesday night.

In Tuesday’s loss to the Dream, Arike Ogunbowale led the way for the Wings as she recorded 20 points and four rebounds while Paige Bueckers added 15 points and three assists of her own.

Now Dallas will try to bounce back on Thursday as they face off against the Lynx in a tough home matchup.

On the other side, Minnesota enters Thursday’s contest with a 1-1 record after defeating the Phoenix Mercury on the road on Tuesday night.

Natasha Howard led the way for the Lynx in Tuesday’s victory as she ended the game with 14 points and 11 rebounds while Olivia Miles recorded 13 points, six rebounds and seven assists.

While both teams are looking to pick up a victory in Thursday night’s matchup, both squads may be without some of their key players due to injuries.

For the Lynx, they have two players listed on their injury report, including star forward Napheesa Collier.

Minnesota has ruled out Collier as she continues to recover from left ankle surgery that she underwent in March and she is expected to be sidelined until early June.

The Lynx have also ruled out Dorka Juhasz due to a right foot injury as she will miss her third consecutive contest to begin the season.

Wings Injury Report: Will Azzi Fudd Play?

On the other side, the Wings have only one player listed on their injury report as they have listed rookie Azzi Fudd as questionable.

Dallas has listed Fudd as questionable due to a right knee injury that caused her to miss Tuesday’s loss to the Dream and she is in danger of missing her second straight game.

Fans can catch Thursday night’s matchup between the Lynx and Wings from Dallas at 8:00 p.m. ET on Amazon Prime Video.
