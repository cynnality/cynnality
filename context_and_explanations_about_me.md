# BIO / MAIN ABOUT (drafting)

I enjoy learning, making things, identifying patterns, and figuring out how systems work.

I describe myself as haaving a beginner's mind. I don't believe learning really ever ends and I like staying open to new ideas and different perspectives. I love diving into new "realms" of learning and discovering new ways of thinking and approaching problems.

Most of the projects I take on include some aspect of providing clarifty to the subject at hand. This involves a process of searching for and identifying what's causing the lack of clarity and then finding ways to bridge 
that gap. I enjoy this process of exploration and learning because it allows me to understand complex systems and concepts in a way that's accessible to everyone.

Communication is just as important to me as technical skills. Every workplace is made up of people with different experiences, ways of thinking, and ways of communicating. I have a major appreciation for those nuances and differences. And I've found that staying aware of those nuances makes for the most effective 
teamwork and collaboration.

Outside of work, I spend much of my time on personal projects, research, and my digital garden, where I document ideas, experiments, and things I'm learning. Meant to serve as a personal archive and a space for me to experiment with information architecture and web technologies.

I'm happiest in environments that value learning, collaboration, thoughtful problem solving, and community over trends or appearances.
