A custom graduation design for a 24"x18" yard sign

_Designed using **CorelDRAW**, to be printed on vinyl_

I recieved this a photo to be used for a ++custom graduation sign++. The customers didn't have many requests or specifications as far as the design but they mentioned the use of the color red and cursive/script writing for the name. While speaking with them, I could tell they more-so excited about celebrating their son's graduation than concerned about the specifics of the design.

---

This was during "graduation season" so I had been taking pictures of graduation signs I saw while driving around day-to-day. I used those pictures to get inspiration for style and layout and worked up this [rough-draft/deconstructed design](asset:deconstructed-design)


I sourced colors from the schools website for details in the graphic elements.
