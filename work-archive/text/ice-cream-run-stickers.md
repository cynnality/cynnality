# Ice Cream Run Stickers
---
This project involved adapting customer artwork to fit the constraints of a pre-manufactured product. Rather than changing the size of the printed piece, I modified the logo itself to create a more balanced and visually effective final result.
---

The customer sent over the [original logo](asset:original-logo) and I immediately realized the design wasn't particularly suited for the size and shape of the sticker. The obvious solution would be to adjust the shape and cut-lines of the sticker itself but the shape and size was pre-determined by the product the stickers were going to applied to. 

---
I really wanted to make them aware of this before printing and give them a solution altogether. The customer didn't mention anything about possibly adjusting the logo to fit the sticker shape but I also knew that an issue like that might not be immediately apparent to everyone. 

This was the [original design in the print-area](asset:fitted-logo) and this is the [reworked design within the print-area](asset:reworked-logo).
