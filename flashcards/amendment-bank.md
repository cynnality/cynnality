## amendment-1 
name: 1st Amendment
year: 
title: Freedom of Expressions
definition: guarantees freedom of speech, religion, assembly, press, to petition the government. It prohibits congress from setting up an official religion for the nation and it protects citizen's rights to hold any religious belief. It also protects the people's right to express themselves through written speech, oral speech, right to peacefully assemble, or to speak against the government.
clauses:
  - establishment-clause
  - free-exercise-clause
  - freedom-of-speech
  - freedom-of-press
  - symbolic-speech
cases:
	- hazelwood-kuhlmeier
	- engel-vitale
	- tinker-desmoines
	- texas-johnson
	- schenck-unitedstates

## amendment-5
name: 5th Amendment
year: 
title: Due Process
definition: sets rules for a concept called eminent domain, which means the federal government can take private land as long as fair compensation is provided. protects the rights of due process to all citizens under federal law (not applied to states here). Protects against things like double jeopardy and self-incrimination. It also sets out rules for the concept of being indicted by a jury.
clauses:
  - due-process-clause
  - self-incrimination
cases:
	- scott-sanford
	- korematsu-unitedstates
	- miranda-arizona
